DELETE YOUR ACCOUNT.

This page explains how to delete your account in Pick1: AI Sports Picks, published by PICK1, on both Android and iOS.

Delete it from inside the app

This is the fastest route and it takes effect immediately. No email, no waiting.

  1. Open Pick1 and make sure you are signed in.
  2. Tap the profile icon in the top right of the board.
  3. Open Edit profile.
  4. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete account.
  5. Read the confirmation, then tap Delete to confirm.

Your account is erased at that moment and you are signed out. The action cannot be undone, and signing up again later creates a new, empty account.

Delete it by email

If you have lost access to the app or cannot sign in, email privacy@pick1.live from the address on the account, with the subject Delete my account. We complete the deletion within 30 days and reply to confirm when it is done.

What is deleted

Everything below is erased permanently, not archived or anonymised:

  • Your account and sign-in credentials, including your email address
  • Your profile, display name and language preference
  • Every pick you tracked, with its stake and outcome
  • Your favourite teams and sports
  • Your notification preferences and the push tokens for your devices
  • Your product-analytics events, which are keyed to your account identifier

What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchase and billing records. Subscriptions are billed by Google Play or Apple, not by us. We keep the minimum transaction record we are required to keep for tax and accounting, normally up to 7 years, under the legal obligation that applies to it. It is no longer linked to a usable account. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription, see below.
  • The public prediction record. The picks the AI published, and whether they won or lost, are the product's own track record. They were never your data, they contain nothing about you, and they stay published.
  • Server logs. Operational logs that may contain an IP address roll off automatically within 30 days.

Cancel your subscription separately

Deleting your Pick1 account does not cancel an active subscription, because the subscription lives with the store that bills you, not with us. Cancel it first:

  • Android: Google Play app, profile icon, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, Pick1, Cancel subscription.
  • iOS: Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Pick1, Cancel subscription.

Get a copy first

Deletion is permanent, so if you want your data before it goes, email privacy@pick1.live with the subject [Privacy Request] Export and we will send a JSON export within 30 days. Ask for the export before you delete; once the account is gone there is nothing left to export.

Questions

Email privacy@pick1.live. Our full Privacy Policy covers what we collect and why.
