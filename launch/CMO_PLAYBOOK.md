# The CMO playbook — aggressive 18-day plan, $0 incremental

**Acting CMO assessment:** with $1K budget and 17 days, you have ZERO
ability to out-spend the competition. You have 100% of the ability to
out-volume, out-engage, and out-repurpose them. Below is the playbook
I'd run if I were CMO of an agency given this brief.

The 7 principles that govern everything:

1. **POST MORE THAN YOU THINK.** Every additional TikTok per day is a
   $0 fresh shot at the FYP. The current 2/day is leaving 4-6/day on
   the table. Cap at 6 to avoid algo throttle.
2. **REPURPOSE EVERY PIECE 6-8×.** One 45-sec TikTok = native TT + IG
   Reel + YT Short + Twitter video + IG Story + FB Reel + LinkedIn
   clip + carousel screenshots + quote-tweet text. ~30 sec extra work,
   8× distribution.
3. **REPLY > TWEET.** Algorithm rewards engagement, not posting. For
   every 1 original tweet, send 3 replies to other sports accounts.
4. **DESIGN FOR THE SCREENSHOT.** Confidence cards, streak counters,
   contrarian-vs-Vegas graphics. Content that gets screenshotted by
   someone else is free distribution.
5. **HIJACK MOMENTS.** Set Tweetdeck alerts on `Pat McAfee, Schefter,
   ESPN, Adrian Wojnarowski`. When a big sports moment hits, be the
   FIRST account in the replies with Pick1's pre-game probability vs
   what happened.
6. **TRADE > BUY.** One paid influencer post = $500-2K. One Lifetime
   Pro trade for the same post = $0. Same outcome.
7. **MEASURE EVERYTHING, KILL FAST.** Day 3-7 channel viability check.
   Day 10 hard reallocation. If a channel isn't producing signups by
   Day 10, kill it and put the budget where it's working.

## The repurposing engine

### One 45-sec TikTok = 8 pieces of distribution

```
Native TT (1080×1920)
    ├─► IG Reel (same vertical 1080×1920)
    ├─► YouTube Short (same)
    ├─► Twitter video (squared 1080×1080 if longer than 60s, vertical otherwise)
    ├─► IG Story (15-sec teaser → "swipe up")
    ├─► FB Reel (same)
    ├─► LinkedIn video clip (founder-only, edited to 60s max)
    └─► Quote-tweet of the best line (text only)
```

### One IG carousel = 6 pieces

```
IG Carousel (10 slides)
    ├─► Twitter thread (1 slide per tweet)
    ├─► LinkedIn carousel post
    ├─► FB carousel post
    ├─► IG Story sequence (split into 4-5 stories)
    ├─► Substack newsletter (consolidated as long-form)
    └─► YouTube community post (top 1-3 slides)
```

### One Twitter long-form thread = 5 pieces

```
Twitter thread (20 tweets)
    ├─► Substack/Medium essay (consolidated)
    ├─► LinkedIn long-form post (founder voice)
    ├─► IG carousel (one tweet per slide)
    ├─► TikTok talking head (founder reads the highlights)
    └─► Email newsletter (sent to existing list)
```

## Honest CMO take — what NOT to do

❌ **Don't buy followers.** Ever. Poisons algorithm + brand integrity.

❌ **Don't post the same content 4 times waiting for it to go viral.**
Post NEW content. Failed virality is data — move on.

❌ **Don't engage in betting Twitter beef.** Touts will try to bait
you. Don't take it. Brand is trust; can't litigate it on Twitter.

❌ **Don't slow down to "make it perfect."** Velocity beats polish at
this stage. A 7/10 post today beats a 9/10 post in three days.

❌ **Don't dilute the brand voice across platforms.** Same tone
everywhere: confident, numeric, transparent. "Calibrated AI."
Founder = real person, brand = AI tool. Keep them distinct.

❌ **Don't try to do every play.** Pick the top 10-15 from this list
that fit founder energy + audience. Execute those daily.
