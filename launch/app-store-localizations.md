# Pick1 — Localized App Store Listings

Your app already ships in 7 languages, but the App Store listing is English-only.
Adding localized **name + subtitle + keywords + description** makes Pick1 rank in
each country's store — typically the single highest-leverage ASO move available.

> In App Store Connect: each version page has a language dropdown (top-right).
> Add a localization, then fill the fields below. Keywords localize per store —
> this is where most of the international ranking gain comes from.

Brand voice matches `Localization/Lang/*.swift`. League acronyms (NBA, NFL…) stay
global; soccer/betting terms are localized to local search behavior.
