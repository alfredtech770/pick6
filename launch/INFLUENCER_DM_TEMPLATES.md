# Influencer DM templates — paid + trade variants

Use these templates when reaching out to sports / sports-betting
influencers. The TIER-3 template (Lifetime Pro trade) was already in
`launch/INFLUENCER_OUTREACH.md`. Below are the paid variants for the
$25K budget tier — mid-tier ($3K) + micro ($300-500).

**Hard rules across all templates:**
1. Personalized first line is non-negotiable. Reference a SPECIFIC
   post they made in the last 7 days. Copy/paste the URL.
2. One ask per DM. Don't bury the ask in jargon.
3. If they don't respond in 7 days, **do not follow up**. One ask,
   one window. Move on.
4. Never DM more than 5 people per hour from the same account
   (IG/TT auto-flag this as spam).
5. For paid offers: lead with the offer up front. Don't make them
   ask "what's the budget."

## TEMPLATE 1 — Mid-tier creator paid offer ($3K, 300-500K followers)

Used for ONE big-hit creator. Single biggest single-shot reach line
in the $25K budget. Vet engagement >2% before DMing (audit their
last 10 posts).

### IG / TikTok DM (max 1000 chars — these platforms truncate)

```
Hey [first name] — saw your [SPECIFIC POST: e.g. "breakdown of the 
Lakers/Nuggets game-3 line movement last Tuesday"]. The way you 
[SPECIFIC THING THEY DID: e.g. "called out the public-money trap on 
the second half"] is exactly the read we'd want in front of our 
audience.

Quick context: I'm one of the founders of Pick1 — AI that calls one 
game a day across 9 sports. Every prediction logged publicly forever, 
wins AND misses. We launch end of May.

I'd love to sponsor one post from you between now and May 31. 
Budget is $3,000 for either:
- One IG Reel + Story sequence  
- One TikTok native upload

Full creative control — we just want you to honestly try Pick1 for 
3-5 days, then post what you actually think. No script.

If you're open to it, happy to send over our deck + sample picks 
so you can decide before committing.

— Noa
pick1.live
```

### X / Twitter DM (shorter)

```
Hey [first name] — your take on [specific recent tweet/thread] was 
sharp. 

Founder at Pick1 here — AI sports predictions, one pick per day, every 
call logged publicly. We launch end of May.

Want to sponsor 1 post from you for $3K between now and May 31. 
Full creative control — try it for 3-5 days, post what you actually 
think. Open to it?

pick1.live · DM me back

### Email (if DM is closed)

Subject: `Pick1 — $3K sponsorship for one honest review`

```
Hi [first name],

Pick1 founder Noa here. Saw your recent [SPECIFIC POST/TOPIC]. The way 
you handle [SPECIFIC THING] is exactly the angle we'd want in front of 
our launch audience.

The pitch: $3,000 for one sponsored post (your platform of choice) 
between now and May 31. We'd ask you to genuinely try Pick1 for 3-5

launching end of May.

I've attached:
- Our 1-pager
- A look at Pick1's last 7 days of picks
- The sweepstakes we're running (so you can share a referral code 
  with your audience that gives them bonus entries)

If you're interested or want to chat live, just reply or DM 
@PICK1sport. If it's a no, no worries — appreciate the time.

— Noa
Founder, Pick1
https://pick1.live

### Negotiation guardrails

- **Anchor at $3K** for the first offer. Don't open higher.
- **Ceiling: $5K** for someone in the 500K-1M range with >3% engagement
- **Walk away if:** they want >$5K, they demand pre-approval of every word, they're already running a competing sportsbook deal, their engagement <2%.
- **Sweetener (free):** offer their followers a custom referral code that gives 50× bonus sweepstakes entries. Costs us $0, makes their post more valuable.

creator's post are attributable.

## Vet checklist (before sending ANY paid offer)

✅ Engagement rate >2% (likes ÷ followers on last 5 posts)
✅ Audience is US-based (or relevant for sports betting)
✅ Posts about sports / betting / fantasy at least 3×/week
✅ NOT currently running paid posts for DraftKings, FanDuel,
   PrizePicks, BetMGM, or any sportsbook (would conflict)
✅ NOT selling their own "premium picks" service
✅ NOT crypto/NFT adjacent (brand mismatch)
✅ Anonymous accounts: PASS unless they have a track record we can
   verify

If any RED, don't send the DM. Move to the next person on the list.

## Response handling

**They reply "interested" / "tell me more":**
Send them the 1-pager (one is in `launch/`), the Pick1 sample picks
(screenshot of pick1.live/tracker), and the offer details. Set a
7-day "decide by" date.

**They counter-offer higher:**
- Mid-tier: hold at $3K. If they're a clear fit and have >500K with
  high engagement, go to $4K max.
- Micro: hold at $400. If they're a clear fit, $500 max.

**They counter-offer lower:**
- Take it. Always.

**They want creative control demands (must approve every word):**
- Walk away. Authentic > coerced.

**They ghost:**
- 7-day window. Then move on, no follow-up.
