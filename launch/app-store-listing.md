# Pick1 — App Store Connect Submission Package

Copy-paste ready. Every field below maps 1:1 to a field in App Store
Connect → App Information / Pricing & Availability / Version Information.

ASO strategy in one line: **claim "AI", "Sports", and "Picks" in the
name; claim "daily" and "leagues" in the subtitle; use the keyword
budget for sport-specific long-tail searches** — that's how you show up
for "NBA picks", "NFL picks", "soccer predictions", etc. without
wasting characters on the obvious words.

---

## App Information

### Name (30-char limit · primary search signal)

> **Pick1: AI Sports Picks**

`22 / 30 characters`

Why this: claims the three highest-volume search terms in the category
("AI", "sports", "picks") inside the brand mark. Apple ranks name
keywords ~3× higher than subtitle keywords, so this is the most
valuable real estate on the listing.

### Subtitle (30-char limit · secondary search signal)

> **Daily picks. Every league.**

`28 / 30 characters`

Why this: scoreboard-bold, on-brand rhythm (the same "PICK SHARPER
TODAY" cadence the in-app hero uses). Claims "Daily" and "League" as
search terms while reading as a benefit promise, not a keyword stuffing.

### Keywords (100-char limit · comma-separated, NO spaces after commas)

> **nba,nfl,mlb,nhl,epl,ufc,f1,tennis,cricket,soccer,football,basketball,parlay,prop,odds,line,bet**

`98 / 100 characters`

Why this set:
- Every word that's NOT in the name/subtitle is fair game. Apple
  treats name + subtitle + keywords as one big bag, but you can't
  repeat words across them — repeating wastes characters.
- Sport-specific long-tail terms are the highest-converting (someone
  searching "NBA picks" has clearer intent than someone searching
  "AI app").
- "parlay", "prop", "odds", "line", "bet" capture sportsbook-adjacent
  search intent without you claiming to be a sportsbook.
- DO NOT add: "pick", "ai", "sports", "daily", "league" (claimed by
  name/subtitle). DO NOT add: "draftkings", "fanduel" (Apple rejects
  for trademark abuse).

## Last-minute pre-submission checklist

When you're at App Store Connect ready to submit:

- [ ] Build uploaded via Xcode Organizer or TestFlight (not just archived locally)
- [ ] Build processing completed (~15 min after upload)
- [ ] Build selected on the version page
- [ ] 1024×1024 marketing icon uploaded (no transparency, no rounded corners)
- [ ] All three required screenshot sizes uploaded (6.9", 6.5", 5.5")
- [ ] Description, keywords, promo text filled
- [ ] Support + Privacy Policy URLs verified (test from a browser)
- [ ] Age rating questionnaire completed (answers above)
- [ ] App Review notes pasted
- [ ] App Privacy questionnaire completed
- [ ] Subscription products created + Introductory Offer (7-day trial) configured
- [ ] Subscription localizations filled in (at minimum English)
- [ ] Tax + Banking info filled in App Store Connect (Apple won't approve paid apps without this)
- [ ] At least one TestFlight internal test on a real device, full flow: install → sign in → see picks → tap a card → trigger paywall → start trial (sandbox account) → manage subscription → sign out → delete account
- [ ] Pipeline lockfile committed, Railway worker running, supabase RLS verified (already done)
- [ ] Build version bumped: `MARKETING_VERSION = 1.0.0`, `CURRENT_PROJECT_VERSION = 1` (already set)
- [ ] Submit
