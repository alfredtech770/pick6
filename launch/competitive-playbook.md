# Pick1 Competitive Playbook
**How to grow the waitlist with a $500–$2K/month budget when Kalshi/Polymarket are spending tens of millions.**

Last updated: 2026-05-11. Built from Meta Ad Library scrape (live ads) + multi-source strategy research.

---

## Executive summary

Pick1's competitive set spends like a category Kalshi (97 active ads), Polymarket (150), PrizePicks (370), Underdog Fantasy (large national TV). Kalshi alone is a now-$11B company with tens of millions in marketing + lobbying budget. **You cannot out-spend any of them.**

The good news: the entire competitive set sells the same thing in slightly different wrappers — *transactional* sports speculation (bets, picks, plays, contracts). Pick1's wedge is that it sells *information* (an AI forecast with a public ledger). That distinction is also the moat — Kalshi is actively being targeted by a class-action law firm for "Kalshi football bets" (we saw the law firm's ads running directly inside Kalshi keyword search results on Meta Ad Library). Polymarket is unregulated for US trading. PrizePicks is fighting state legality battles.

Pick1's small budget forces three disciplines that actually work in this market:
1. **AI-generated UGC creative** at $50–$200/spot instead of $50K celebrity shoots.
2. **Niche distribution** — Whop cappers, Reddit, sports-betting podcasts at $250–$1,500 / sponsorship instead of broad Meta/TikTok prospecting.
3. **Founder-led public proof** — post the model's pick before the game and the result after, every game, forever. Free, compounds, and is *exactly* the brand that anti-gambling-but-pro-stats sports fans want to follow.

Below: what each competitor is actually doing (verified via live Ad Library), then Pick1's tactical 90-day plan.

## Competitor teardown

### Kalshi — 97 active ads, $11B valuation, the giant

**Funded firepower.** Series C $185M (Jun 2025) → Series D $300M → Series E $1B (Dec 2025 at $11B valuation). Active monthly users 600K → 5.1M in 2025. "Significant portion" of capital going to marketing + lobbying.

**What's actually running in Meta Ad Library right now (verified live):**
- New York Post sponsored content native ads ("Kalshi is the first federally regulated exchange where you can legally trade on anything…")
- App-install ads with **$10 BONUS code NYPOST10** and code LINESTAR (influencer-specific referral codes)
- Product ads using live market data as hook: *"Trade on gas prices. Traders give it a 41% chance gas hits $4 this summer"*
- Founder/podcast video ads (Tarek Mansour)
- Audience targeting: skews young, FOMO, increasingly female (women 13% → 26% of users in 10 months)

**Their most steal-able tactic — the $2K AI ad.** PJ Accetturo's NBA Finals spot:
- Tools: Google Veo 3 + Gemini for prompt expansion
- Time: ~3 days
- Cost: **~$2,000 all-in**
- Reach: 20M+ impressions, ran on Disney/ESPN primetime
- Workflow: rough script → Gemini turns shots into Veo 3 prompts (max 5 at a time) → 3 parallel Veo windows → 300–400 generations to get 15 usable clips → final edit
- Tone direction: *"GTA VI meets Florida Man"*

**Distribution plays we can't copy:** CNN ticker, CNBC editorial integration, AP partnership, Chicago Blackhawks deal, Fox Sports talent — these are BD/PR, not paid.

**Stunts we won't copy:** Westside Market free-grocery NYC giveaway, $50K WaPo print + DC billboard "we ban insider trading and death markets" anti-Polymarket campaign, "Kalshi Girls" X account, college ambassadors (publicly torched for the last one).

**🚨 Strategic vulnerability:** A class-action law firm (Lieff Cabraser Heimann & Bernstein, LLP) is running Meta ads INSIDE Kalshi's keyword search results: *"Bad experience with Kalshi football bets? You're not alone — learn more today."* This is the entire reason Pick1's "we don't take, place, or facilitate any wagers" positioning exists. Lean into it hard.

### Polymarket — 150 active ads, the crypto-native challenger

**Spend.** ~$1M on US Meta ads in fall 2024 (election-driven). 2025–26 push framing US legality: *"BREAKING: Legal football trading is coming to ALL 50 states this fall."*

**What's actually running right now:**
- Dedicated **"Polymarket Sports" sub-brand FB Page** running separate creative
- **$20 BONUS code FREE20** (2x more aggressive than Kalshi's $10)
- Cultural-moment hooks: *"Think you know who's winning Artist of 2026? Check the live odds and pick your winner on Polymarket"* (Spotify Wrapped equivalent)
- Lifestyle framing: *"Your favorite team. Your favorite artist. Your favorite moment. If there's a winner, there's a market."*
- US legality push targeted at illegal-gambling states

**Distribution differentiator:** Polymarket is now the **official prediction market of X (formerly Twitter)** via the xAI partnership. Equivalent of free top-of-feed distribution to all of sports/political Twitter. Golden Globes integration. Coplan (founder) drives Twitter brand voice — "competitive shitposting" with Kalshi is part of the brand.

**Net difference vs Kalshi:** Kalshi spends like a regulated US fintech (TV, OOH, primetime). Polymarket spends like a crypto-native consumer brand (X-native, partner-led, founder-content-led, less paid).

### PrizePicks — 370 active ads, the DFS giant

**The biggest active ad presence of all three.** Every ad has the same hook: *"Make real money player picks in your state."* Geographic legality is their #1 talking point because they're constantly fighting state-by-state regulatory battles.

**Creative formula:** Celebrity + comedy pairings via Preacher (Austin) + Klutch Sports.
- "It's Good To Be Right" campaign: Adam DeVine + Marshawn Lynch, juxtaposing fake-business failures ("Devinity" cologne, "Yeast Mode" cookbook) with the dopamine of being right
- Druski + Reggie Bush "Working Out" spot
- Heavy OOH near stadiums

**Audience overlap with Pick1:** This is the closest direct overlap — sports fans who think they know better than the market. The DFS audience is the EXACT Pick1 prospect. Studying PrizePicks creative is more valuable than studying Kalshi creative for your hook patterns.

### Underdog Fantasy — celebrity-led DFS

- Bill Belichick spots ($5 → $100 bonus offers)
- Nelly spots
- "We Only Need One Yard" — promo-led, conversion-focused
- Celebrity cameo + bonus formula

**The DFS lesson for Pick1:** This audience responds to (1) humor, (2) recognizable face, (3) "being right" framing — NOT "betting" framing. Pick1 sits closer to this brand voice than to Kalshi's "trade everything" pitch.

## Pick1's wedge — the underserved segment everyone is missing

**The bet:** Kalshi, Polymarket, PrizePicks, Underdog all sell *action*. Their copy is `bet`, `pick`, `play`, `trade`. The unaddressed segment is the **sports fan who wants an edge but doesn't want to gamble**.

This segment is bigger than the gambling segment:
- 50%+ of NFL fans don't bet
- ~74% of NBA fans don't bet
- Women are 51% of sports fans but only 26% of Kalshi users (per their own data)
- Sports fans in non-legal states want intellectual engagement
- Older fans want analytics, not action

**Concrete differentiation:**

1. **Lean into "we don't take your money."** Kalshi's biggest brand vulnerability is being called a gambling app while denying it. Be the opposite: *"Pick1 is a forecasting tool. We don't run the bets."* This is also what the law firm angle protects against.

2. **Public track record as the core marketing asset.** Every X post, TikTok, Reddit comment = a timestamped prediction + result. Free, defensible, compounding. Polymarket does this and gets it for free.

3. **Target the non-bettor.** Women, older fans, non-legal-state fans, NBA Athletic-reader-style analytical fans.

4. **AI-forecast voice, not absurdist voice.** Kalshi owns "chaos." Pick1 owns *"the model said so. The model was right."*

5. **One sport, one model, one proof point at launch.** Don't market as "everything." Pick NFL or NBA, run the model publicly for one season, build receipts. Expand once one sport is proven.

## Tactical playbook — $500–$2K/month, 90 days

### Channel allocation (recommended)

| Channel | Monthly budget | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Meta Ads (cold prospecting)** | $300–$600 | Already set up (Campaign 1 draft built). Use this to validate creative + CPL benchmarks. |
| **Meta Ads (retargeting)** | $100–$200 | After 7 days of cold traffic, retarget site visitors who didn't sign up. CPL typically 3–5× cheaper. |
| **TikTok Ads** | $0 in month 1 (organic only) → $200–$500 month 2+ | Organic founder content first. Paid only after winning organic format. |
| **Whop / Discord capper sponsorships** | $200–$500 | Sponsor 1–2 capper community daily-pick threads per week. $100–$250 each. |
| **Sports betting podcast micro-sponsorships** | $0–$500 | Skip Pod Save / All-In tier. Target 5K–50K download shows at $250–$1,500 a read. |
| **Tools (referral, analytics)** | $30–$100 | KickoffLabs / Waitlister for referral mechanic. Mixpanel free tier for funnel. |

### Creative production — the Veo 3 / Sora workflow

This is the most leveraged tactic in the entire playbook. Kalshi made a $2K AI ad that hit 20M impressions on national TV.

**Setup:**
- Google Veo 3 access (via Google AI Studio) OR Sora (via ChatGPT Pro/Plus)
- ChatGPT for prompt expansion
- CapCut or Premiere for final edit
- Budget: **$50–$200 per finished 15-second spot**
- Cadence: **3–5 new spots per week** = 12–20 monthly = ~$1,500/mo all-in

**Pick1's tone direction (parallel to Kalshi's "GTA VI meets Florida Man"):**
- *"FiveThirtyEight meets ESPN30for30"* — analytical confidence, prediction reveal, result confirmation
- *"A model with attitude"* — Pick1's AI has receipts and isn't shy
- *"Quiet conviction"* — the opposite of Kalshi's chaos; we *know* before the game

**Sample concepts (steal these for the first round):**

1. **"The Pick Before The Game"** — Pre-game shot of an NBA stadium, voiceover: *"Pick1's model gave Boston 73% to cover. The game tipped off three hours ago."* Cut to final score. *"Boston covered by 9. We told you."* 15 seconds.

2. **"What Vegas Got Wrong"** — Vegas line on screen. Pick1's confidence next to it. *"Vegas had this at -3.5. We had it at -7. The final was -10. Subscribe to see tomorrow's calls before they happen."*

3. **"The Receipt Wall"** — Camera pans across a virtual wall of timestamped predictions, alternating green ✓ and red ✗. *"Every prediction. Every result. Public. We don't delete the losses."*

4. **"Founder asks the model"** — Founder on camera (UGC style, vertical 9:16, phone-shot): *"I asked Pick1's model who wins Lakers/Celtics tonight. Here's what it said."* Cut to confidence dial filling. *"73% Celtics. Watch the game with me — I'll post the result either way."*

### Founder-led organic distribution

**X (Twitter)** — post weight: **HIGH**. Free.
- 1 thread/day: tomorrow's high-confidence picks + reasoning
- 1 post/day: yesterday's results (wins AND losses — the wall of receipts is the brand)
- 1 reply/day: jump in on game-day Twitter with the model's pick before the game
- Cadence: 21 posts/week. Spend: $0.

**TikTok** — post weight: **MEDIUM**. Free.
- 1 video/day: pick + reasoning, 15s
- 1 video/day: result + lesson, 15s
- Pre-recorded in batches. ~2 hours of recording/week = 14 videos.

**Reddit organic** — post weight: **HIGH** for waitlist conversion. Free.
- r/sportsbook (1M+), r/sportsbookadvice (200K+), r/fantasyfootball (1M+), r/nbadiscussion (800K+)
- **Don't post links.** Post the model's weekly pick log as a comment in game threads.
- Drop link to pick1.live only in your profile and in DMs when people ask.
- This is exactly the niche Kalshi can't touch with paid because Reddit suppresses gambling brand ads.

### Waitlist referral mechanic

Robinhood signed up 1M users on $0 paid via line-jump referrals. Implement the same.

- Tool: **KickoffLabs** ($29/mo) or **Waitlister** ($15/mo)
- Mechanic: every signup gets a unique referral link. 1 referral = +50 places. 5 referrals = unlock early access (Day 1 vs Day 7). 10 referrals = free month at launch.
- Promote in the WhatsApp confirmation message + welcome email + thank-you page

This is purely additive on top of the existing Brevo signup flow — just adds a column for `referral_code` and an outbound URL.

### Sports betting podcast micro-sponsorships

The 30-second pre-roll read on a 5K–50K-download sports betting podcast is the best $300 you can spend pre-launch. Hosts vouch for products to a hand-raising audience.

**How to find them:**
- Podscribe.com (search "sports betting" by audience size)
- Magellan.fm (creator marketplace)
- Or just DM your top 20 favorite sports-analytics podcasters directly

**Pitch template:**
*"Hi [host], building an AI sports prediction app called Pick1. Public ledger, no betting. Pre-launch — looking to sponsor a $250–500 read on a 1–2 episode test. DM me if interested?"*

## What to AVOID at this budget

- ❌ Broad Meta/TikTok prospecting at <$1K/mo (CPMs inflated by Kalshi spend; you lose)
- ❌ Celebrity/athlete creative (PrizePicks burned ~$2M on Marshawn Lynch; you can't compete)
- ❌ "I made $500 overnight" UGC hooks (FTC risk; Kalshi can afford lawyers, you can't)
- ❌ OOH or CTV (sub-scale, untrackable at this budget)
- ❌ College ambassador programs (Kalshi got publicly torched; same tar would hit Pick1)
- ❌ Massive podcast deals (Pod Save / All-In / Rogan reads = $50K+; not at this stage)
- ❌ Trying to outrun Kalshi/Polymarket on cash bonuses ($10/$20 freebies; they'll win every time)

## 30-day launch checklist (when payment is resolved + FB Page is created)

**Week 1 — Validate creative**
- [ ] Publish Campaign 1 (LEAD-US-broad-20260511, $30/day) with 3 ad variants (1 Veo 3 video + 2 static)
- [ ] Founder posts 7 X threads + 7 TikToks + 5 Reddit comments
- [ ] Target CPL: $4–$8 (cold US, broad audience)
- [ ] Day 7: pause ads with CPL >$15, scale ads with CPL <$5

**Week 2 — Add interest targeting**
- [ ] Build Campaign 2 (LEAD-US-interest, $30/day): NBA/NFL/MLB interests + DraftKings/FanDuel interests-of
- [ ] Sponsor 1 Whop capper community ($250 test)
- [ ] Sponsor 1 sports betting podcast ($300 test)

**Week 3 — Retargeting + creator content**
- [ ] Build Campaign 3 (LEAD-US-retargeting, $20/day): site visitors who didn't sign up
- [ ] Ship the referral mechanic (KickoffLabs)
- [ ] Founder books 2 podcast guest spots (give, not buy — appear on shows as guest)

**Week 4 — Scale winners, kill losers**
- [ ] Double budget on the top-CPL ad set; pause anything CPL >$10
- [ ] Add lookalike audience (1% LAL of waitlist signups — need 100+ source events first)
- [ ] First "wall of receipts" social asset: a 30-day public ledger PDF/landing page

**Target by Day 30:**
- 500–1,500 waitlist signups
- Validated winning ad concept
- 1 podcast partnership locked
- 100+ X followers, 500+ TikTok followers
- CPL trending toward $5–$8

## When to reconsider this plan

- **If Meta CPL stays >$15 after 2 weeks** → kill paid Meta, double down on organic + Whop sponsorships
- **If TikTok organic videos hit >10K views consistently** → kick in TikTok paid ads (Spark Ads format — promotes your organic posts)
- **If a Whop sponsorship beats Meta CPL** → reallocate 50%+ of Meta budget to Whop
- **If founder podcast/X traction outpaces paid** → you've found product-market-fit for the brand. Drop paid acquisition entirely until launch.
- **If Kalshi/Polymarket sue you** (unlikely but plausible if you grow loud) → that's free PR. Don't settle, lawyer up, lean into "the prediction market players are scared of the forecasters."
