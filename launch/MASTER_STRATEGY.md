## 1 · THE BET

> **A calibrated AI that calls one game per day across 9 sports, with every pick — wins AND misses — publicly logged forever, can beat every entrenched player (Kalshi, Polymarket, PrizePicks, ESPN touts) on the one metric that matters: trust.**

We're not competing on volume of picks, on bigger payouts, on flashier interface. We win on **calibration and transparency**. The rest of the market lies. We don't.

### Why now
- Kalshi raised $185M and burns $50M+/yr on Veo-3 NBA-Finals ads — they validated demand for "AI-style sports predictions" without competing on calibration
- Polymarket has $1B+ volume but useless for sports (illiquid, slow line moves)
- Sports tout industry is a $X00M cesspool of cherry-picked "guaranteed locks"
- AI is finally good enough to do this well — the model that powers Pick1 wouldn't have been possible 18 months ago

### What we're not
- **Not a sportsbook.** No bets. No money on the platform.
- **Not a tout service.** We don't sell "premium picks." We don't promise to "guarantee a winner."
- **Not gambling.** We are a **predictions and analytics tool**, compliance-clean by design.

## 9 · VIRAL ACTIVATIONS (the 5 plays we have to nail)

### 🔥 Activation 1 — Position-Jumping Referral (LIVE)
- **What:** Every signup gets `pick1.live/?r=<code>` — each successful referral moves them up 100 positions
- **Tiers:** 3 refs = 1 month free / 10 refs = 1 year free / 25 refs = lifetime Pro
- **Why it works:** Position is social currency; sports bettors share picks reflexively; reward is infinitely scalable for us (zero fulfillment cost)
- **Status:** ✅ Built, deployed, tested end-to-end
- **Expected lift:** 15-20% of signups come via referral (viral coefficient ~0.3)

### 🔥 Activation 2 — Public Accountability Bet (Week 2 launch)
- **What:** Pick1 publicly commits: "If our locks (⭐⭐⭐ picks, 80%+ confidence) don't win at ≥60% over the next 30 days, every paid user's first month is refunded."
- **Why it works:** Forces attention, signals confidence, removes risk for new users, news-worthy. Sports Twitter eats this stuff.
- **Spec:** Public dashboard showing running lock record; auto-honor refunds; one-shot press push
- **Risk:** If we lose, we lose money + face. Calibration says we shouldn't. Worth the bet.

### 🔥 Activation 3 — "Beat the AI" Challenge (Week 3 launch)
- **What:** Public Twitter/IG game. Each day, AI posts its pick; followers post theirs. Running scoreboard at pick1.live/beat-the-ai. End of week leaderboard winner gets a year of Pro free.
- **Why it works:** Self-fueling viral loop. Every participant tweets THEIR pick, which mentions @pick1, which exposes their followers, who can join.
- **Spec:** Hashtag #BeatPick1, simple Twitter API tracker, weekly winner DM, public leaderboard page on site

### 🔥 Activation 4 — Influencer Trade Program (Week 1 launch, runs through)
- **What:** DM 20 sports betting micro-influencers (5K-50K followers). Offer: free year of Pro + early access + custom referral code that gives THEIR audience 1 month free. In exchange: one honest 7-day review post.
- **Why it works:** Free for us, valuable for them, audience-aligned. Even 3 yeses = 5K-150K eyeballs.
- **Status:** Outreach list to be built day 7
