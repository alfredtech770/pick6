PICK1 SUPPORT.

Need help with Pick1: AI Sports Picks? Email hi@pick1.live and a real person replies, usually within one business day. Tell us your device and what happened.

Subscription and billing

Pick1 Pro is billed by Apple or Google, not by us, so changes are made in your store account.

  • Cancel or change plan on iPhone: Settings, your name, Subscriptions, Pick1.
  • Cancel or change plan on Android: Google Play app, profile icon, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions, Pick1.
  • Refunds: Apple handles App Store refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com. For Google Play, request it from your Play order history.

Restore your purchase

New phone, or Pro not showing? Sign in with the same account, open the paywall and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID or Google account that made the purchase. If it still does not work, email us with the date of purchase.

Notifications

If picks or alerts stop arriving, check that notifications are allowed for Pick1 in your phone settings, then open the app once so it can refresh your device. Followed teams and alert types are managed in the app.

Picks and results

Pick1 is an AI analysis app, not a sportsbook. Picks are forecasts with a probability, never a guarantee. How the model works and how every result is graded is explained on the Method page. If you think a result was graded wrong, email us the game and we will check it.

Your account and data

  • Delete your account: in the app, or by email. Full steps on the delete account page.
  • Privacy requests: email privacy@pick1.live. See the Privacy Policy and Terms.

Play responsibly

Pick1 is for adults and for information only. If sports betting stops being fun, help is free and confidential: in the US call or text 1 800 GAMBLER, in the UK visit BeGambleAware, in France call 09 74 75 13 13 (Joueurs Info Service).
