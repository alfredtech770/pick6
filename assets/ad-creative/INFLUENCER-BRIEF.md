## What Pick1 is (say it this way)
Pick1 is an **AI sports analytics app**. It publishes **predictions** with a **confidence score** on every game, across 9 sports, and grades its entire track record on a **public ledger — wins AND losses**.

## What Pick1 is NOT (never blur this)
It is **not a sportsbook**. It doesn't take, place, or facilitate wagers of any kind. You are advertising an analytics/research app, the same way you'd advertise a stock-research app — not a way to make money.

## THE GOLDEN RULE
**Never talk about money you (or anyone) won.** All results language belongs to the app's public record:
- ✅ "The model hit 72% last month — the ledger's public."
- ❌ "I made $500 last weekend." / "This paid for my vacation."

One personal-winnings claim is enough for Meta to reject the ad and flag the account. This is the #1 reason sports apps get banned from advertising.

## 🚫 BANNED WORDS — never say these on camera or in captions

**Gambling vocabulary** (any of these re-classifies the ad as gambling):
bet / betting / wager / gamble / sportsbook / bookie / betslip / parlay / odds / the line / spread / moneyline / over-under / cover / units / bankroll / juice / vig / chalk / fade / tail / sweat / action / stake / capper / handicapper / degen / "plays" (as in betting plays) / "pool" (as in money pool)

**Money-promise vocabulary** (reads as an income claim):
guaranteed / lock / sure thing / can't lose / free money / easy money / get rich / profit / ROI / winnings / payout / cash out / "pay your rent" / "quit your job" / any dollar amount tied to a result

**Clickbait vocabulary** (Meta's sensational-content filter):
"they don't want you to know" / "secret" / "loophole" / "hack" / "before it gets banned" / "illegal"

The scripts are beat sheets, not lines to recite. Rewrite the wording in your own voice — your slang, your pacing, your humor. Keep three things exactly as written: (1) the hook idea, (2) which app screen shows at each beat, (3) every rule on this page. Scripted-sounding UGC dies in the feed; your version of the line will always outperform ours. If you change a line, run the new wording past the banned-words list before filming.

## 🔢 NUMBERS MUST MATCH THE APP
Before filming, check the live ledger in the kit footage and say **the number it currently shows** — don't recite "72%" from this brief if the app says something else that week. Every stat you say on camera must be visible on screen behind you. If in doubt, say less: "the whole record is public" is always safe and always true.

## 🎯 EXACT-SCORE CALLS — special rules
The app sometimes calls the exact final score, and the graded "EXACT SCORE HIT" card is fair game — it's real, timestamped, and graded in-app. But:
- Only show a **real** exact-score hit from the Footage Kit (Clip H) — never a mockup or hypothetical
- Say **"it called this one exactly"** — never "it predicts exact scores" as a general ability. Exact hits are rare; presenting them as routine is exactly the claim Meta bans
- Every exact-score video must include the honesty beat: **"this is rare — most picks it just calls the winner, and the misses are graded publicly too"** with the ledger on screen
- Never attach money to it ("imagine if you'd played that…" = instant rejection)

## 🎣 HOOKS & RETENTION — how these convert

**The first 1.5 seconds decide everything.** Rules:
- Open mid-thought, already talking — no "hey guys," no intro, no logo
- The hook must create a question the viewer needs answered ("it posts its losses… *on purpose?*")
- Don't say the app name in the first 5 seconds — name it only at the CTA, or they scroll before the payoff
- Show the most surprising screen (the ledger with a LOST badge) within the first 3 seconds
- One idea per video. If a sentence doesn't push toward the CTA, cut it
- Talk 10% faster than feels natural; cut every breath and pause in the edit
- Captions burned in, word-by-word — most viewers watch muted
- End on the CTA, hard cut, no outro ("First week's free — link below.")

**Deliver each script 3 ways:** film your assigned script once, then re-film just the first 3 seconds with two alternate hooks from the bank below. Three hooks × one body = three ads for Meta to test.
