# Pick1 UGC Scripts — Round 1 (Kalshi-style influencer videos for Meta)

> ⚠️ SUPERSEDED — use `ugc-greenscreen-scripts.md` + `INFLUENCER-BRIEF.md` instead. This skit-format version is kept for reference only and has NOT had the final language audit (it still contains "pick'em pool" / "units" phrasing that was scrubbed from the green-screen pack).

10 scripts, each with hook, beats, CTA, and shot notes. Compliance rules at the bottom — send those to every influencer with their script.

**The one rule that keeps you alive on Meta:** the influencer NEVER talks about money they won. All results language belongs to the app's public ledger ("the model hit 72% last month"), never the person ("I made $2k"). Personal-winnings claims are what get sports-adjacent ads banned. The app's track record is a product fact; "I got rich" is a prohibited earnings claim.

---

## S1 — Street Interview ("Beat the AI")
*Format: Kalshi-style man-on-the-street. 30–45s. Highest production, highest ceiling.*

**HOOK (0–2s):** "We asked 50 strangers to out-predict an AI. It did not go well for the strangers."
*On-screen: "50 HUMANS vs 1 AI"*

**BEATS:**
- (2–15s) Rapid cuts: "Who wins tonight, Celtics or Knicks?" — 3–4 confident wrong answers, one guy way too confident. Comic timing over polish.
- (15–22s) Cut to phone: "Pick1's model had it at 84% before tip-off." Show confidence dial. "Final score:" — freeze frame on the result.
- (22–30s) Back to the over-confident guy: "You said Knicks by 20." Him: "…I did say that."
- **CTA (30s+):** "It's called Pick1. AI predictions across 9 sports, and every pick — right or wrong — is on a public ledger. First week's free."

**Shot notes:** Real reactions beat scripted ones. Get releases. Film 20+ interviews, use the funniest 4.

**Alt hooks:**

## S3 — The Receipts Angle
*Format: talking head + screen recording. 20–30s. Trust play — likely your best converter for cold traffic.*

**HOOK (0–2s):** "This sports app posts every time it's wrong. On purpose."
*On-screen: "IT POSTS ITS LOSSES??"*

**BEATS:**
- (2–10s) "Every picks guy on the internet deletes his misses and screenshots his wins. Pick1 does the opposite — every prediction gets timestamped to a public ledger. Wins AND losses. You can scroll the whole history."
- (10–18s) Screen recording: scroll the ledger, deliberately pause on a LOSS. "See? That one missed. It's still there. That's the point."
- (18–24s) "Last 30 days it hit 72%. I don't have to trust it — I can check it."
- **CTA:** "Pick1. Predictions with receipts. First week's free."

**Why this works for compliance:** transparency framing is the anti-scam signal Meta's reviewers (and users) respond to. Showing a loss on screen is your credibility AND your policy shield.

## S4 — The Girlfriend Who Doesn't Watch Sports
*Format: couple skit. 20–30s. Broad-audience play, softens the "betting bro" read.*

- (10–18s) Her: "He watches four hours of pregame shows. I check one confidence score." Shot of the app, confidence dial.
- (18–24s) Him: "It's genuinely humiliating." Her: "It's genuinely 9 sports."
- **CTA:** Her: "Pick1. First week's free. Tell your boyfriend I'm sorry."

**Alt hook:** "My boyfriend studies film all week. I press one button and win our house pick'em."

## S7 — Silent Demo (POV)
*Format: pure screen capture + captions, no face. 15s. Cheapest possible test; runs as native feed content.*

**HOOK (0–2s):** On-screen text: "What an 84% confidence pick looks like at the final buzzer."

**BEATS:**
- (2–12s) Screen recording: open app → tonight's slate → tap a game → confidence dial at 84% → cut to live win-probability bar climbing in-game → final score matches.
- On-screen captions carry the story: "AI trained on 1.2M games" → "9 sports" → "every result public — even the misses."
- **CTA card:** "Pick1 — first week free."

**Why include it:** zero influencer risk, fastest to iterate, and Meta's algo often surfaces silent demos cheapest. Run it alongside every influencer batch as the control.

# COMPLIANCE SHEET — send to every influencer

## Never say / never show
- ❌ Any personal winnings: "I made $500," "paid my rent with it," cash, Venmo/bank screenshots
- ❌ "Guaranteed," "lock," "can't lose," "free money," "easy money," "get rich," "print money"
- ❌ Betting verbs as the product: "bet with it," "parlay," "tail my slip," betslip screenshots, any sportsbook app on screen
- ❌ "Quit my job," lifestyle-flex framing (cars, stacks, "financial freedom")
- ❌ Anything implying viewers currently gamble or have a problem ("for my degens," "chasing losses?") — personal-attributes violation
- ❌ Naming competitors/tipsters, or anyone under 21 on camera

## Always
- ✅ Results language belongs to the APP's public ledger, never the person: "the model hit 72% last month," not "I hit 72%"
- ✅ Say or show a loss/miss in every video that mentions accuracy
- ✅ "It's analytics / predictions / a forecast" framing — never "betting service"
- ✅ Paid-partnership label on (Meta branded-content tool), and disclose #ad
- ✅ End-card + caption disclaimer (template above)
- ✅ 21+ audience targeting on every ad set
