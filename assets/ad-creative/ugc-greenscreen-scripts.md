# Pick1 UGC Scripts — Green-Screen Format (Kalshi-style, for Meta)

Format: influencer talks over app footage — either **face** (green-screen effect, creator in front of the app, pointing at UI) or **faceless** (voiceover + screen recording + bold captions). Every script below works both ways; faceless = same VO, captions carry the energy.

**The one rule that keeps you alive on Meta:** results language always belongs to the APP's public record ("the model hit 72% last month"), never the person ("I made $2k"). Personal-winnings claims, betting verbs, and cash imagery are what re-classify you into Meta's gambling category. Full compliance sheet at the bottom — send it with every script.

# COMPLIANCE SHEET — send with every script

## Never say / never show
- ❌ Personal winnings: "I made $500," "paid my rent," cash, bank/Venmo screenshots
- ❌ "Guaranteed," "lock," "can't lose," "free money," "easy money," "get rich"
- ❌ Betting verbs as the product: "bet with it," "tail my slip," "parlay" — no betslips, no sportsbook apps on screen, ever
- ❌ The POTENTIAL PAYOUT / POTENTIAL RETURN screens — do NOT include them in green-screen footage (that's why influencers only use the approved Footage Kit)
- ❌ Implying viewers gamble or have a problem ("for my degens") — personal-attributes violation
- ❌ Real tipster names/handles/logos; anyone under 21 on camera

## Always
- ✅ Results belong to the APP: "the model hit 72%," never "I hit 72%"
- ✅ A LOST badge visible in every video that mentions accuracy (Clip E)
- ✅ "Analytics / predictions / forecast" framing — never "betting service"
- ✅ Paid-partnership label + #ad disclosure
- ✅ End-card + caption disclaimer (template above); 21+ targeting on every ad set
